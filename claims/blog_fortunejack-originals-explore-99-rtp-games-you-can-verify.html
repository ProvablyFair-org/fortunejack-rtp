What 99% RTP actually means

Theoretical Return to Player (RTP) represents the mathematical percentage of total wagered stakes returned to players across millions of rounds.
A 99% RTP corresponds to a 1% theoretical house edge. Across a massive statistical sample of $10,000 in total stakes, the mathematical model retains approximately $100 and returns about $9,900. This metric reflects broad long-term averages rather than a short-term promise for individual sessions.

How Provably Fair results can be verified

Provably Fair technology uses cryptographic algorithms to confirm that game outcomes are determined randomly and without manipulation. Before a round begins, the system generates a server seed and presents its SHA-256 hash. The player’s browser supplies a client seed, and a nonce tracks total rounds played. After the round, the unhashed server seed is revealed. Combining these three inputs through the verifier algorithm reproduces the exact outcome shown on screen.

A four-step verification walkthrough

You can audit any completed round result directly in the game interface:

  1. Open the panel: Click the fairness icon or history tab on the control bar.
  2. Copy seed data: Copy the active Server Seed, Client Seed, and Nonce values.
  3. Open the verifier: Launch the FortuneJack verifier tool or an independent SHA-256 calculator.
  4. Compare results: Paste the seed values into the fields to confirm the recalculated outcome matches your result.
