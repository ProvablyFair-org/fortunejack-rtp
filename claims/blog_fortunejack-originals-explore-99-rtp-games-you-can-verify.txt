 How Provably Fair results can be verified 
Provably Fair technology uses cryptographic algorithms to confirm that game outcomes are determined randomly and without manipulation. Before a round begins, the system generates a server seed and presents its SHA-256 hash. The player’s browser supplies a client seed, and a nonce tracks total rounds played. After the round, the unhashed server seed is revealed. Combining these three inputs through the verifier algorithm reproduces the exact outcome shown on screen.
